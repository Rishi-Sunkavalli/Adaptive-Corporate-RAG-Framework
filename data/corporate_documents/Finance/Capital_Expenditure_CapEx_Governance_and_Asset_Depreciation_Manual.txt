## 1. Capital Expenditure Definition and Capitalization Floor
Tangible assets or software development expenditures with an acquisition cost of $5,000 USD or greater and an estimated useful operational lifespan exceeding 12 months must be capitalized as fixed assets rather than expensed as operating expenditure (OpEx). Items under $5,000 are expensed immediately.

## 2. CapEx Budget Authorization Matrix
CapEx projects between $50,000 and $200,000 require Capital Budgeting Committee approval and a Return on Investment (ROI) payback period under 36 months. Projects exceeding $500,000 require Board of Directors Audit Committee ratification.

## 3. Asset Depreciation Schedules
Depreciation is recognized using the straight-line method over statutory useful lives: Computer Hardware & Servers: 3 years (36 months); Enterprise Software Licenses: 5 years (60 months); Office Furniture and Fixtures: 7 years; Leasehold Improvements: Lesser of 10 years or remaining lease term.
