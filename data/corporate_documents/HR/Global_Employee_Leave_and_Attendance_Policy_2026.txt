## 1. Purpose and Scope
This policy establishes standard corporate standards governing annual leave, medical absence, parental leave, bereavement, and public holidays for all permanent full-time and contracted employees across all worldwide subsidiaries. All leave requests must be logged through the Corporate HRMS portal.

## 2. Working Hours and Core Collaboration Window
Standard operational hours are 40 hours per week, Monday through Friday. Core collaborative business hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local office time, during which all active employees are expected to be reachable via Slack and email unless pre-approved for flexible asynchronous schedules.

## 3. Annual Leave Entitlement
Regular full-time employees accrue 22 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accruing monthly at the rate of 1.83 days per completed month of service. A maximum of 8 unused annual leave days can be rolled over into the succeeding calendar year. Any carried-over leave must be fully utilized prior to March 31st; unutilized rollover balances are permanently forfeited without financial encashment.

## 4. Sick and Medical Leave
Employees are granted 12 days of fully paid medical and sick leave per annum. Any sick leave extending beyond two consecutive working days requires the submission of a medical certificate issued by a licensed medical practitioner within 48 hours of returning to duty. Medical leave does not roll over to subsequent years.

## 5. Parental, Maternity, and Paternity Leave
Primary caregivers (maternity leave) are entitled to 18 weeks of fully paid leave upon the birth or legal adoption of a child under 5 years old. Secondary caregivers (paternity leave) are entitled to 4 weeks of fully paid leave. Employees may request an additional 4 weeks of unpaid parental leave with HR Director approval.

## 6. Compassionate and Bereavement Leave
Employees may take up to 5 consecutive paid working days for the loss of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling). For extended relatives (grandparent, in-laws), 3 paid working days are granted. International travel allowances for bereavement require People Partner clearance.
