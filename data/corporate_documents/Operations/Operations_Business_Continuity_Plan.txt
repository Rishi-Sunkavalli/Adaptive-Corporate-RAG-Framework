## 1. Executive Purpose
The Enterprise Business Continuity Plan (BCP) ensures the uninterrupted continuation or rapid resumption of mission-critical business functions during severe operational disruptions, natural catastrophes, cyber-attacks, or physical facility outages.

## 2. Business Impact Analysis (BIA) and Recovery Objectives
- Mission-Critical System Tier 0 (Core Payment Processing, Customer Identity Vault):
  * Maximum Tolerable Downtime (MTD): 4 hours.
  * Recovery Time Objective (RTO): 2 hours.
  * Recovery Point Objective (RPO): 15 minutes.
- Business-Essential System Tier 1 (Customer Support Ticketing, CRM, Telephony):
  * MTD: 12 hours; RTO: 6 hours; RPO: 1 hour.
- Internal Productivity System Tier 2 (Intranet Wiki, HR Portal, Expense Filing):
  * MTD: 48 hours; RTO: 24 hours; RPO: 24 hours.

## 3. Emergency Crisis Command Structure
- Crisis Management Team (CMT): Led by the Chief Operating Officer (COO), with representatives from Security, IT Infrastructure, HR, Legal, and Corporate Communications.
- Alternate Incident Command: If the primary HQ location is physically compromised, the CMT convenes virtually via an encrypted secondary Zoom bridge or off-site emergency operations center.
- Activation Authority: The BCP may be officially invoked by the CEO, COO, or VP of Global Security.

## 4. Workforce Displacement and Remote Failover
- In the event of a regional office closure (e.g., weather emergency, power grid failure), all employees in the affected territory are instructed to switch to remote work protocols.
- Cellular emergency notifications are dispatched via Everbridge within 20 minutes of incident declaration.
- Employees requiring satellite connectivity or temporary co-working passes (WeWork/Regus) are granted immediate approval through the Operations Emergency Fund up to $500 per week.

## 5. Annual Testing and Simulation Drills
- Tabletop exercises: Conducted quarterly by departmental leads to simulate ransomware scenarios and supply chain halts.
- Full unannounced functional failover drill: Executed annually to validate database replication, secondary DNS failover, and employee notification responsiveness.
