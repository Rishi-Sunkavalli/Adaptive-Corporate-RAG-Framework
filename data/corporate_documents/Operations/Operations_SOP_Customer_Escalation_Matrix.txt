## 1. Objective and Tiered Support Model
This Standard Operating Procedure establishes the support hierarchy, triage protocols, and escalation timelines to resolve client-reported incidents swiftly and preserve client trust.
- Tier 1 (Customer Frontline Support): First-contact triage, basic configuration assistance, known error FAQ resolutions. Handles 70% of inbound inquiries.
- Tier 2 (Technical Escalations): Deep investigation of application logs, database queries, API webhook failures, and integration diagnostics.
- Tier 3 (Sustaining Engineering & Product Teams): Code-level hotfixes, platform patch deployments, and core architecture investigations.
- Tier 4 (Executive Incident Management): COO, VP Customer Success, and Legal involvement for catastrophic contract breaches or multi-tenant system degradation.

## 2. SLA Response and Resolution Target Windows
- Severity 1 (Enterprise Blocker):
  * First Response Time: 15 minutes.
  * Workaround or Mitigation: 2 hours.
  * Permanent Resolution: 8 hours.
  * Executive Alert: COO and VP Customer Success notified within 30 minutes of ticket creation.
- Severity 2 (Major Feature Impact):
  * First Response Time: 1 hour.
  * Workaround or Mitigation: 6 hours.
  * Permanent Resolution: 24 hours.
- Severity 3 (Minor Operational Inconvenience):
  * First Response Time: 4 business hours.
  * Permanent Resolution: Within standard sprint cycle (up to 10 business days).

## 3. Escalation Triggers and Reassignment Rules
A ticket must be escalated immediately to the next tier under any of the following conditions:
1. Two consecutive failed diagnostic attempts by Tier 1.
2. 50% of the customer's contractually agreed SLA window has elapsed without identifying a verified root cause.
3. Customer explicitly requests management escalation ("Karen trigger").
4. Financial impact exceeds $10,000 per hour of continued downtime.

## 4. Client Communication Protocol During Outages
- In the event of a Severity 1 incident, external client updates must be published to status.company.com every 30 minutes.
- All public communications must adhere to pre-approved legal templates. Engineers and support agents are strictly forbidden from attributing root causes to third-party providers (e.g., "AWS is down") until confirmed in writing by the Incident Commander.
