## 1. Standard Contract Terms and Governing Law
All corporate commercial agreements, customer contracts, and vendor master services agreements (MSAs) must adopt the approved corporate standard legal templates. Governing law for US operations is the State of Delaware; for EMEA operations, governing law is the courts of England and Wales. Modifications to governing law require General Counsel sign-off.

## 2. Limitation of Liability and Indemnification Caps
Corporate policy stipulates that aggregate liability under any commercial contract shall not exceed 12 months of total fees paid or payable by the customer under the applicable Order Form. Super-caps for data breach or gross negligence are restricted to a maximum of 2x the standard annual fee cap.

## 3. Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure Term Duration
Mutual non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) must mandate confidentiality protection for a minimum period of 5 years following disclosure of proprietary business information. Trade secrets, source code, and customer records must be protected in perpetuity without expiration.
