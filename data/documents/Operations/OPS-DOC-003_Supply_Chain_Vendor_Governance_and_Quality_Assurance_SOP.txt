## 1. Vendor Performance Scorecards and Quarterly Reviews
Critical tier-1 suppliers are evaluated quarterly across four operational dimensions: On-Time Delivery (minimum 98.0% threshold), Quality Defect Rate (maximum 0.05% reject rate), Pricing Transparency, and Customer Service Responsiveness. Suppliers scoring below 85% composite score are placed on 60-day corrective action plans.

## 2. Warehouse Inbound Inspection and Quarantine Procedures
All incoming hardware shipments must undergo physical inspection at the inbound quarantine staging dock within 4 hours of receipt. Barcode serial scanning, seal verification, and sample testing of 5 units per pallet must be logged in the Warehouse Management System (WMS) prior to release to inventory.
